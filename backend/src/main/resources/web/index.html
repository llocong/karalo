How it works

Ready to sing in three steps

Karalo runs on the TV. Your guests use the phone they already have.

1

Open Karalo on your TV

A QR code appears on the screen. Search and play songs with the remote, or with your voice.

2

Guests scan the code

They type their name and they're in, from any phone and any network. Nothing to download.

3

Pick songs and sing

Songs join a shared queue and play one after another. Everyone sees what's coming up next.

Features

Everything a karaoke night needs

Nothing it doesn't. No microphones to pair, no accounts, no setup.

A song for every singer

A huge, ever-growing catalogue of karaoke tracks in every genre, from this week's hits to timeless classics, in English, French and more.

Join with a QR code

Guests scan the code on the TV and add songs from their phone's browser. Nothing to install, no Wi-Fi password to share.

A shared, live queue

Every phone and the TV see the same queue, live, with who's singing each song. Drag to reorder, swipe to remove.

Control from any phone

Pause, resume or skip a song from your phone. No need to hunt for the remote mid-chorus.

The party runs itself

Songs play back to back. When the queue runs dry, the TV shows the QR code so the next singer can jump in.

Made for the big screen

A TV app built for the remote, with voice search and ready-made picks. Play a song right away without losing the queue.
